This is a brand-led replacement for the portrait-led hero. It is intentionally
under review and is not yet an approved cross-platform master. The local site
uses it so the owner can judge the real responsive composition before an asset
is promoted into `asset-manifest.json`.

## Direction

- Mountain West ridge resolves into a loose `M` silhouette.
- One Warm Amber switchback and waypoint express progress and experience.
- Signal Cyan contours and sparse network geometry connect the health and
  evidence themes without using a literal brain or medical symbol.
- Cloud paper provides an inviting first viewport and usable copy space.
- No person, face, text, logo lockup, gradient, medical cross, ECG trace, DNA,
  microphone, watermark, or dark overall field.

## Generation Record

Method: OpenAI built-in image generation, then local WebP export at quality 88
with metadata stripped.

Prompt summary: create a wide, high-key editorial identity landscape combining
precision screenprint and technical engraving; place an intentional mountain/M
and ascending path on the right half; preserve the left half as quiet off-white
copy space; use only Midnight, Signal Cyan, Warm Amber, Cloud, and supporting
slate; avoid portraits, literal anatomy, stock scenery, glossy 3D, gradients,
glow, purple, and embedded text.
