# Logo Directions - Identity Round 01

- Status: **review concepts, not approved masters**
- Authority: [`../../media-design-guide.md`](../../media-design-guide.md)
- Review sheet: [`logo-directions-review-sheet.svg`](logo-directions-review-sheet.svg)

This round isolates the logo decision from the website redesign. Every
direction uses the exact display name `DR. M EXPERIENCED`, the exact host line
`with Dr. David Musnick`, Geist Sans, and only the established Midnight,
Signal Cyan, Warm Amber, Cloud, and supporting slate colors. The marks use no
gradient and avoid medical crosses, caduceus symbols, ECG traces, brains,
microphones, and stock medical imagery.

The lockups keep live Geist type so the owner can review the intended wordmark
relationship while the concept remains editable. After one direction is
selected, convert the approved type to outlines and build the complete stacked,
one-color, light/dark, circular-crop, and pixel-adjusted export family. Do not
use these files on the website or any public channel before that approval gate.

## Comparison

| Direction | Core idea | Strength | Primary risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 - Switchback | One continuous `M` route with an amber waypoint | Open, human, and immediately legible at 16 px | The familiar monoline construction is less ownable than the other marks |
| 02 - Cutline | Solid `M` with a path climbing through negative space | Strongest emblem and clearest premium identity | The internal route can close when poorly rasterized below 24 px |
| 03 - Waypoint Steps | Terraced `M` with an amber experience marker | Most robust small-size geometry and easiest one-color production | The modular form can feel technical or digital rather than warm |

The amber element is intentionally small in every full-color mark. Removing it
does not remove the core silhouette; each concept also has a dedicated Midnight
one-color file. The 16, 24, and 64 pixel samples on the review sheet are placed
at their true SVG sizes. Review the sheet at 100% zoom before judging the
favicon result.

## Exact Owner Approval Questions

1. Which direction should advance: `01 - Switchback`, `02 - Cutline`, `03 - Waypoint Steps`, or `none`?
2. Does the selected mark read first as a distinctive `M` and forward path, without reading primarily as an outdoor-recreation brand?
3. Should the small Warm Amber waypoint remain in the full-color identity, or should the production mark be Signal Cyan only?
4. Is the hierarchy of `DR. M EXPERIENCED` over `with Dr. David Musnick` approved as shown?
5. May the selected direction be expanded into the complete outlined horizontal, stacked, one-color, dark/light, and pixel-adjusted logo family for a second approval round?

An answer to these five questions selects a direction; it does not authorize a
website change, platform update, asset-manifest approval, or public rollout.
